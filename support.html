Getting started

Audio Router is a menu-bar app for macOS 14.2 or later. Click its cable icon near the system controls to open the routing panel, or right-click the icon for the app menu and Settings.

Complete onboarding, grant the audio access you need, and start the 7-day trial or restore Lifetime Unlock. Add an application or input device as a source, then connect its cable to an output device. A source can connect to more than one output.

Choose the profile you want to edit before changing routes. Start at a comfortable volume; headphones are a useful first output when routing a microphone. Settings → Audio contains latency, connection animation, and routing preferences.

Audio access is missing

Routing another app’s audio needs macOS system audio capture access. Routing a microphone or another input device needs microphone access. Open Settings → Privacy in Audio Router to check access and find the relevant system settings.

In System Settings → Privacy & Security, allow Audio Router in the microphone or system audio recording category as appropriate. Category names vary with macOS version. If macOS asks you to quit and reopen the app, do so, then check access again.

Select an application again with the app picker if it was moved, renamed, or replaced and its saved source no longer resolves.

A route has no sound

  • Check that the source application is playing audio or that the input device is receiving a signal.
  • Confirm that the intended profile is selected and its cable connects the source to the correct output.
  • Check the source, connection, and output volume and mute controls, including the destination’s controls in macOS or on the device.
  • Check the route’s status message, audio permissions, and trial or Lifetime Unlock status in Settings → About.
  • If an output disconnected, reconnect it and allow the saved route to recover. If the problem remains, disconnect and reconnect the affected cable or quit and reopen Audio Router.

If microphone feedback protection muted a route, lower the volume or choose headphones before reconnecting. When reporting a silent route, tell us whether the source meter moves and whether other routes still work.

Devices, latency, or audio quality

If a device is missing, first check that it is connected and appears in macOS Sound settings or Audio MIDI Setup. Reconnect USB or Bluetooth devices and check any driver or virtual-device software they require. Audio Router’s own internal routing and metering devices are hidden from the device list.

For crackling, dropouts, or unstable Bluetooth playback, turn off Low Latency Mode in Settings → Audio to use the recommended buffering and recovery headroom. Compare the same source through another output to narrow down the problem.

If the sound differs from what you expect, check the connection’s filter settings, gain, mute, delay, and ducking controls. Temporarily bypass processing to compare. For split routes, tell us which destination is affected, the devices’ sample rates, and whether the issue follows a device reconnect or sleep and wake.

Trials, purchases, and restoration

Audio Router offers a manually started 7-day trial and a one-time Lifetime Unlock. The trial does not automatically renew or charge you. When access expires, routing stops while saved profiles and settings remain available to view and edit.

To recover a purchase, sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for it, connect to the internet, and choose Settings → About → Restore Purchase. Restoring a trial preserves its original dates and does not start a new trial.

Previously verified access can be remembered for offline use. Initial purchases and restoration require Apple’s services. If the App Store is unavailable, retry while online and include the displayed error message when contacting support.

Apple handles payments and refunds. Follow Apple’s refund instructions to request a refund. We never need your Apple Account password or payment card details.

GET IN TOUCH

Still need help?

Email potatoplanet.studios@gmail.com for troubleshooting, feedback, feature requests, or privacy requests.

For a problem report, include:

  • Your Audio Router version, shown in Settings → About, and your macOS version.
  • The source type and output device types, plus whether one or all destinations are affected.
  • What you expected, what happened, the steps to reproduce it, and any error shown.
  • Whether the issue follows a reconnect, sample-rate change, or sleep and wake.

We may ask for additional diagnostic information to help investigate a problem. Review anything you choose to share. Nothing is automatically sent to us. Do not send audio recordings or personal information unless they are needed for your request.

You can also report a bug on GitHub. GitHub Issues are public and require an account. Use email for private requests and keep private device names, file paths, and purchase records out of public issues.

See our privacy policy for how support messages are handled, and data deletion instructions for help clearing local data.
